High Chances of Asthma

#### Asthma

Risk of Asthma symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms including intense wheezing, severe shortness of breath, significant chest tightness, and persistent coughing that may disrupt daily activities.

Do's :

- Avoid going outside and keep windows closed to reduce exposure to pollutants.

- Take prescribed medications as directed by your healthcare provider.

- Maintain clean indoor air with air purifiers, especially in bedrooms and living areas.


Don'ts :

- Smoke or expose yourself to secondhand smoke.

- Engage in physical exertion outdoors.


![heart due to air pollution in New Delhi](https://www.aqi.in/media/sections/health-advice/disease-heart.svg)

High Chances of Heart Issues

#### Heart Issues

Risk of Heart Issues symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms including intense chest pain, severe fatigue, irregular heartbeats, difficulty breathing, and symptoms that worsen with minimal physical activity.

Do's :

- Stay indoors and avoid outdoor activities to minimize exposure.

- Keep prescribed medications readily available and take them.

- Maintain a calm environment and avoid stressful activities.


Don'ts :

- Perform heavy lifting or exercise.

- Ignore symptoms like chest pain, shortness of breath, or palpitations.

- Smoke or expose yourself to pollutants.


![allergy due to air pollution in New Delhi](https://www.aqi.in/media/sections/health-advice/disease-allergy.svg)

High Chances of Allergies

#### Allergies

Risk of Allergies symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms including intense coughing, throat soreness, airway irritation, significant nasal congestion, chest tightness, and itchy eyes.

Do's :

- Keep windows closed and avoid going outside when AQI is high.

- Take allergy medications as prescribed by your doctor.

- Use air purifiers and regularly vacuum with HEPA-filter vacuums to reduce indoor allergens.


Don'ts :

- Go outside without a mask.

- Burn firewood or use smoky appliances.

- Leave windows open during high pollen seasons.


![sinus due to air pollution in New Delhi](https://www.aqi.in/media/sections/health-advice/disease-sinus.svg)

High Chances of Sinus

#### Sinus

Risk of Sinus symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms, such as a runny nose with thick mucus, facial pressure, teeth pain, fever, cough, headache, and tiredness.

Do's :

- Check the air quality index regularly to make decisions.

- Use a humidifier to add moisture to the air and help clear sinuses.

- Stay indoors and avoid outdoor air pollution exposure.


Don'ts :

- Smoke or expose yourself to outdoor smoke.

- Skip your prescribed medicines for symptoms.


![flu due to air pollution in New Delhi](https://www.aqi.in/media/sections/health-advice/disease-flu.svg)

High Chances of Cold/Flu

#### Cold/Flu

Risk of Cold/Flu symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms including persistent cough, significant nasal congestion, high fever, chills, fatigue, sore throat, and headaches.

Do's :

- Stay indoors as much as possible and keep windows and doors closed.

- Use saline nasal sprays to clear nasal passages and reduce congestion.

- Seek medical advice if symptoms worsen or do not improve within a week.


Don'ts :

- Ignore severe symptoms or delay seeking medical attention.

- Use alcohol-based mouthwash excessively.

- Open windows for ventilation when AQI is high.


![copd due to air pollution in New Delhi](https://www.aqi.in/media/sections/health-advice/disease-copd.svg)

High Chances of Chronic (COPD)

#### Chronic (COPD)

Risk of Chronic (COPD) symptoms is High when AQI is Severe (150-301)

Severe symptoms such as intense shortness of breath, chronic coughing, significant chest tightness, wheezing, and extreme fatigue.

Do's :

- Stay indoors and keep windows closed when AQI is high.

- Use an air purifier to Ensure indoor air is clean.

- Follow your doctor's advice and take medications as prescribed.


Don'ts :

- Be around smoke or strong odors.

- Use a fireplace or wood stove.
